AI infrastructure

Three AI agents do the heavy lifting. People make the calls.

AGENT 1

Expertise Ingestion

Turns each member's background into a structured profile, sets skill levels from evidence, and shows where the coalition's skills fall short of the work.

AGENT 2

Grant-Writing

Gathers project details from the hubs, checks eligibility against federal and private funders, builds the proposal, and tracks milestones for awarded grants.

AGENT 3

Resource Allocation

Validates every hub request against its project budget, matches members to the work, and releases funds only after a named person approves.

How a resource request moves

  1. 1 · Submit
    The hub names the project, resources, timeline and milestone.
  2. 2 · Validate
    The request is checked against the project budget. Mandatory.
  3. 3 · Approve
    A named person decides. The budget is checked again first.
  4. 4 · Release
    Funds are committed and members are assigned.

Governance

AI that answers to people.

A person approves every AI output

Answers and drafts wait for a named reviewer, who approves, edits or rejects them. The AI can never approve its own work.

Members consent first

No one is profiled or matched without saying yes. Profiles hold no contact details, demographics or pay, and withdrawing consent deletes them.

The math isn't the AI's

Budgets, eligibility and scores come from tested calculations. Any number the AI states is checked against them.

Everything is on the record

Every AI call, decision and approval is logged, and a kill switch can turn the AI off at any time.
